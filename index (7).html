How it was built

01LoadDfE SEN2 statutory data: plans at January, new plans, requests and 20-week timeliness, recognised by their columns.
02Analyse in SQLWindow functions for growth, a CTE with RANK() for a national league, joins for the request-to-plan pipeline and neighbour comparisons.
03Test the modelsSix simple models backtested on every council in England. The lowest typical error wins, and the range comes from real past errors.
04ExplainForecasts turned into annual reviews and school places, with a plain-English brief for senior managers.
